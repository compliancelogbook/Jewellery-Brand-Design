# Shop by Category verification

The desktop Shop control opens a focused product-category mega-menu with direct links to Rings, Earrings, Necklaces, Bracelets, and Bridal Accessories. It is available by both focus/click and hover behavior, while themed edits remain independently available in the primary navigation.

The direct Rings route and Bridal Accessories route render category-specific merchandise. Mobile checks confirm the category page hero and two-column product cards are contained, legible, and not reliant on the desktop mega-menu; categories are exposed in the mobile navigation structure.
