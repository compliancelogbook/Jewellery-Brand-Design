# Saved pieces verification

On the Solis Ridge Ring page, selecting **Save for later** updated the product control to **Saved to your pieces** and incremented the desktop navigation heart count from 0 to 1.

Opening the navigation heart displayed the saved ring with its image, material, price, removal control, product link, and the account invitation explaining that browser-local saves can later be synchronised through an account.

After closing the drawer and navigating to The Bridal Edit, the saved-piece count remained at 1 and the ring card still showed its saved-state heart, confirming browser-local persistence across routes.

The mobile Bridal Edit preview confirms that heart controls remain visible on product cards without interfering with product imagery, labels, or pricing.
