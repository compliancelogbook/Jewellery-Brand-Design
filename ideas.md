# Orielé — Design Direction

## Reference Ground-Truth Spec

The supplied reference, `victoriapercival.com`, establishes the target **layout grammar**, not a source of content or brand assets. The Orielé implementation will preserve the following observable design logic: a narrow promotional ticker above a transparent-over-hero header; a full-bleed editorial image hero with restrained white type and two compact action buttons; alternating product rails, editorial collection panels, and soft-neutral breathing space; a recognisable luxury-commerce hierarchy built around imagery before information; and a dark, utility-forward footer. All names, photography, product designs, copy, logo treatment, editorial story, product data, endorsement marks, and visual assets will be independently created.

## Chosen Approach — Mediterranean Keepsake Modernism

**Design Movement:** Contemporary resort editorial with the cut-paper spontaneity of Italian travel ephemera and understated fine-jewellery merchandising.

**Core Principles:** The structure stays image-led and horizontally expansive; product information remains quiet and centred beneath imagery; stark black product ground is used only to isolate metallic objects; and larger editorial panels create pause between commerce-driven moments.

**Color Philosophy:** Chalk (`#F5F3EE`) provides a sun-bleached gallery field; Ink (`#12100D`) makes metal and typography feel precise; Saffron (`#D5A240`) supplies a controlled warm glint rather than a generic gold wash; Sea Glass (`#8CA9A1`) is a cool counterweight; and Salt White (`#FEFEFC`) keeps text crisp over dark photography. The ownable brand color is **Orielé Saffron** (`#D5A240`).

**Layout Paradigm:** A full-bleed, vertical editorial procession. The site shifts between edge-to-edge scenes, four-across commercial rails, and split-screen collection interludes rather than reducing all content to a single central container.

**Signature Elements:** A small radiant-orbit mark, hairline divider rules with a sun-star waypoint, and high-contrast black product still-life grounds recur throughout the interface.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Navigation becomes opaque only after it leaves the hero; product cards gently reveal a second image and a concise action on hover; rail arrows move by a single considered card interval; all movement is short, softly eased, and optional for reduced-motion users.

**Animation:** The hero image enters without theatrical effects. Text layers lift by 8px with a 280ms soft ease. Editorial panels use a once-only opacity-and-translate reveal as they enter the viewport. Product images scale to 1.025 on pointer hover with 260ms easing. Reduced motion disables all entrance motion and carousel transitions.

**Typography System:** `DM Serif Display` carries editorial headlines at 400 weight with tight optical tracking. `Manrope` is used for navigation, product information, and utility labels in 400–600 weight, often in all caps with measured tracking. Headline sizes are deliberately large on wide screens but use clamp-based reduction on mobile.

**Brand Essence:** Orielé is modern keepsake jewellery for people who collect occasions, not trends. **Poised, sun-warmed, discerning.**

**Brand Voice:** The voice is intimate, observant, and tactile rather than overtly promotional. Headlines name a feeling or occasion; action labels are calm, specific verbs. Example lines: “Objects of a well-kept summer.” and “Find the piece that remembers the light.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** The wordmark is a custom-feeling serif wordmark set with generous spacing, paired with a compact orbit-and-ray symbol. The mark appears prominent in navigation and the footer but never competes with the hero image.

## Style Decisions

The reference uses a beach-like, light-forward visual language. Orielé keeps the layout cadence and commerce priorities but shifts the material universe to a chalk, ink, and saffron keepsake palette. It will not reuse any reference photography, product imagery, collection names, visual marks, testimonials, publication logos, or customer reviews.

The Orielé wordmark and orbit-and-ray mark are elevated into the persistent header and the home hero signature rather than being confined to the footer. **Orielé Saffron** is now systematically used in the orbit mark, ticker waypoints, the hero signature, product labels, decorative sun-star divider, and craft numerals. Image treatment is constrained through a softened warm filter, with editorial and product images prioritising charcoal, chalk, warm stone, muted sea-glass and controlled gold. The two-up home editorial section was rebalanced so its headline and copy occupy dedicated negative space below the paired images instead of overlaying the imagery.

### Journal extension — AW/26

The Golden Hour Journal is a separate editorial reading room, not a second shopfront. Its direction is **Couture Correspondence**: an original, quiet, French-fashion-editorial sensibility expressed through black silk, chalk stone, silver, pearl and ink-blue accents. It does not imitate any fashion house, campaign, logo, or publication. The signature device is a **folio margin** — small issue, season and reading-time labels that frame the imagery as an observed design note rather than a sales message.

The Journal page uses a deliberately asymmetric hero with one large fashion portrait and a narrow title column, then a paced sequence of three considered AW/26 jewellery signals: sculptural silver, unsentimental pearls, and celestial linework. The homepage will not receive a new full-scale content block; instead, a compact ink ribbon after the craft band will offer a single “Read the Journal” invitation. This preserves the landing page’s product-led rhythm while giving editorial content a clear home.

**Style Decisions:** Golden Hour London remains the approved temporary primary house brand throughout the header, footer, commerce and Journal experiences; “Golden Hour Journal” is an editorial designation rather than a replacement brand. The orbit mark and a cool mineral waypoint appear in the Journal folio and margin device to connect it to the wider system without introducing gold or yellow typography, which is explicitly excluded from the brand treatment.
